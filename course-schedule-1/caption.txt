Courses are locked doors. Prerequisites are the keys.

Course Schedule I asks one question: can every course be completed? This walkthrough follows CourseSchedule1.java and explains Kahn's topological sort through directed edges, indegrees, a ready queue, and one final count.

See why duplicate prerequisites need consistent counting, why a diamond waits for both branches, and how a cycle can block courses that are not themselves cyclic. Expected time is O(numCourses + prerequisites.length).
